Colour

Green means “go and get it”. Purple is reserved for the two actions that ask a pharmacist for something — requesting stock and holding stock. Red is for failure states only, never decoration.

Typography

Body copy never drops below 13 px — most users are on small, low-brightness phones, often outdoors. Headings are Poppins; everything else is Inter.

Shape, spacing and elevation

Everything is a multiple of 4, skirting 8. Shadows are deliberately faint — heavy shadows read as desktop UI and cost frames on low-end Android.

Buttons

Primary buttons are 48 px tall and full width. Purple is only for “Request stock” and “Reserve” — the two moments the patient asks the pharmacy for something.

Variants

States and sizes

Disabled uses #D0D5DD with #98A2B3 text — never a faded brand colour, which still looks tappable.

Icon buttons

40 px square is the minimum reliable touch target on the budget Android devices in the pilot.

Chips, badges and freshness

Every stock chip carries an icon and a word, so the state survives colour blindness, greyscale screens and bright sunlight.

Stock chips

Low stock text uses #A66A05 rather than #F5A524 — the raw amber fails contrast on the pale tint.

Components

Rendered live from the same modules the prototype uses, so this page can never drift from the app.

Pharmacy card

Name, distance, open/closed tag, PCN badge, stock chip and a freshness label — the six facts a patient needs before they decide.

Reservation banner

Purple card with a live countdown. Both actions sit on the card so the patient never has to scroll to act.

Bottom nav

Floating white pill. The active icon sits in a green rounded square; inactive icons stay outline-only.

Prescription notice

Required on every listing, per the PRD. It also keeps MedFinder clearly outside prescribing and payment.

Icons

Rounded outline icons on a 24 px grid, stroke 2, drawn at 20–24 px. Filled only when active. No doctor photography anywhere — illustrations use pharmacy objects (pills, mortar, map pins) in mint or purple tints.
